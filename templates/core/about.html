Our Story

Leading the Way in Environmental Excellence

Founded in Kigali, WMRS (Waste Management & Recycling System) started with a simple yet powerful mission: to revolutionize how waste is managed in urban and rural Rwanda.

What began as a small collection service has evolved into a comprehensive environmental solutions provider. We don't just collect waste; we manage the entire lifecycle—from smart collection and segregation to professional recycling and processing.
